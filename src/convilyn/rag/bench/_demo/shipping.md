# Shipping

## When an order leaves

Orders placed before two in the afternoon on a working day leave the shop
the same day. Later orders, and orders placed at the weekend, leave on the
next working day. Books that the shop has to order from a publisher leave
when they arrive, usually within ten days, and the order page shows the
expected date.

## Delivery times and costs

| Service | Time after dispatch | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard post | three to five working days | free over 40 dollars, otherwise 4 dollars |
| Tracked courier | one to two working days | 9 dollars |
| Collection from the shop | ready in two hours | free |

Tracked courier deliveries need someone to sign for them. If nobody is home,
the courier leaves a card and tries again the next working day.

## Sending abroad

The shop posts to most countries. International orders go by tracked
airmail, which takes seven to fourteen working days. Import duties and local
taxes are charged by the destination country and are paid by the customer
on delivery; the shop cannot pay them in advance.

## Changing the address

The delivery address can be changed on the order page until the order
leaves the shop. After that, contact the courier with the tracking number.
