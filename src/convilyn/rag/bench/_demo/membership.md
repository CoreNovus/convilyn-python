# Membership and points

## Joining

Membership is free. Sign up at the counter or on the website with an email
address. Members get a card, or can show the membership number on their
phone.

## Earning and spending points

Members earn one point for every dollar spent, in the shop or online. Points
appear on the account the day after the purchase. One hundred points are
worth five dollars off a later purchase, and can be used in the shop or at
online checkout.

Points are not earned on gift cards, on postage, or on the part of a
purchase paid with points. A returned book takes its points back with it.

## How long points last

Points expire twelve months after they were earned, unless the member makes
any purchase in that time, which keeps all points for another twelve months.
The account page shows the date the oldest points will expire.

## Reading club

Members can join the monthly reading club for free. The club meets on the
first Thursday of each month at seven in the evening, upstairs in the shop.
The book for the next meeting is ten percent off for club members.
