# Returns and refunds

This handbook covers the bookshop's counter and its online orders. It was
written for the bench demo and describes no real shop.

## Returning a book

A book bought in the shop or online can be returned within 30 days of
purchase, with the receipt or the order number. The book must be in the
condition it was sold in: no writing, no torn pages, and the dust jacket
still on if it came with one.

Gift receipts are accepted. The person returning a gift receives store
credit rather than money back, because the gift receipt does not show how
the book was paid for.

Books that were special-ordered for one customer cannot be returned unless
they arrive damaged. Magazines, maps and opened puzzle sets cannot be
returned at all.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account used for the purchase. A card refund
takes five working days to appear on the statement; the shop starts it the
same day the book comes back. Cash purchases are refunded in cash at the
counter, up to the amount on the receipt.

Online orders returned by post are refunded once the parcel reaches the
shop. Return postage is paid by the customer unless the book was damaged or
the wrong book was sent.

## Damaged or wrong books

If a book arrives damaged, or a different book arrives, write to the shop
within 14 days with a photo. The shop sends a prepaid return label and
posts the right copy before the damaged one comes back. There is no need to
wait for the refund before the replacement ships.
